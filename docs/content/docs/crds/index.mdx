---
title: CRD reference
description: Every kind the operator adds, its fields, and an interactive manifest builder.
---

Each page lists a kind's `spec` fields, shows a minimal example, and has a
form that builds a ready-to-apply manifest. These pages are generated from
the CRDs themselves, so they always match the installed version.

| Kind | Short names | Scope | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`AngosLocalInstance`](/docs/crds/angoslocalinstance) | `ali` | Namespaced | An Angos registry the operator runs in this namespace: its `Deployment`, its configuration (rendered from this spec, its repositories and identities) and the objects around them. |
| [`AngosRepository`](/docs/crds/angosrepository) | `arepo` | Namespaced | A repository of an Angos instance: the namespaces under its `prefix`, who may pull, browse, push and delete there, where it caches from and replicates to. |
